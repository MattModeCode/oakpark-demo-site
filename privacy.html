Privacy policy

Oak Park receptionist demo is a personal prototype used by one person, its developer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the developer's consent it reads and writes one Google Calendar and one Google Sheet that it created in the developer's own Google account. It uses nothing else in the account.

What it stores

An access token is kept in a file on the developer's own computer. Appointment details entered while testing are written to that calendar and sheet, which only the developer can open. The prototype uses made-up test patients.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else. Google user data is not used for advertising or to train any model.

Contact and removal

Questions or deletion requests: 1chinmat@gmail.com. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account permissions page.
